# Prime Kids — Android Feature List

> **For the marketing team** — Everything shipping with the Android app.

---

## Child Safety & Monitoring

### Screen Time Management
- **Daily device limits** — Set a total screen time cap per day (e.g. 2 hours). When the child reaches the limit, a friendly full-screen message appears and the device becomes unusable until the next day.
- **Per-app time limits** — Set individual time limits for specific apps (e.g. 30 minutes of YouTube per day). The app is blocked once the limit is reached.
- **Scheduled device-off periods** — Block the device entirely during set hours (e.g. bedtime 10 PM–6 AM, school hours 8 AM–3 PM). Supports different schedules for different days of the week.

### App Blocking & Approval
- **Automatic new app detection** — When the child installs a new app, it is immediately blocked and the parent receives a notification with the app's name and icon.
- **One-tap approve or block** — From the parent app, approve or block the new app with a single tap. Blocked apps are greyed out and completely inaccessible.
- **Age rating badges** — Each app approval request shows its age rating (3+, 7+, 12+, 13+, 16+, 17+, 18+) so the parent can make an informed decision.
- **Full installed apps list** — View every app installed on the child's device with icons, names, and usage data.

### Web Content Filtering
- **Category-based blocking** — Block harmful websites across multiple categories: adult content, gambling, violence, drugs, weapons, hate speech, malware, phishing, social media, gaming, and streaming.
- **Works across all browsers and apps** — Filtering works at the network level, so it covers Chrome, Firefox, in-app browsers, and any other app that accesses the internet.
- **Custom blocked domains** — Add specific websites to block (e.g. a particular social media site).
- **Custom allowed domains** — Whitelist specific websites that the child needs for school or research, even if the category is blocked.
- **Browsing history** — See which websites the child visited, which were blocked, and when.

### Real-Time Location Tracking
- **Live location on a map** — See the child's current location on an interactive map at any time.
- **Location history trail** — View where the child has been throughout the day with a movement trail on the map.
- **Address lookup** — Tap any location point to see the street address.
- **Open in Maps** — One tap to open the child's location in Google Maps for directions.
- **On-demand location refresh** — Request an immediate GPS update from the child's device.

### Geofencing (Safe Zones)
- **Create safe zones** — Draw zones on the map around places like home, school, or grandma's house with a custom radius (100 m, 200 m, 500 m, or 1 km).
- **Entry and exit alerts** — Get instant notifications when the child enters or leaves a safe zone.
- **Multiple zones** — Set up as many safe zones as you need.
- **Activate or deactivate** — Turn individual zones on and off without deleting them.

### SOS Emergency Button
- **One-press emergency alert** — The child holds the SOS button for 2 seconds to send an emergency alert to the parent.
- **GPS location included** — The alert includes the child's exact GPS location so the parent knows where they are.
- **Visual confirmation** — The child sees a clear "Alert Sent" confirmation on screen.
- **Priority notifications** — SOS alerts stand out with a red highlight and pulsing indicator in the parent's alert list.

### Remote Device Control
- **Lock the device** — Lock the child's phone remotely with one tap. A full-screen "Device Locked" message appears and the child cannot use the phone.
- **Unlock the device** — Unlock remotely when ready.
- **Force location update** — Request the child's current GPS position on demand.
- **Force data sync** — Trigger an immediate sync of all activity data.

## Parent App Experience

### Dashboard
- **All children at a glance** — See all your children listed with their online/offline status.
- **Subscription status** — View your active plan, days remaining, and expiration date.
- **Alert counter** — See how many unread alerts need your attention.
- **Pending approvals** — See how many new app requests are waiting.
- **Quick access** — Tap any child to view their full activity details.

### Activity Reports
- **Daily, weekly, and monthly views** — Switch between time periods to see usage trends.
- **Screen time charts** — Visual bar charts showing daily screen time with clear day labels.
- **Top apps ranking** — See which apps the child uses most, ranked by time with usage bars.
- **Web activity log** — Browse the child's web history with timestamps and blocked/allowed indicators.
- **Blocked attempts summary** — See how many times content was blocked, broken down by day.

### Smart Alerts
All alerts are categorized and filterable:
- **Screen time limit reached** — The child hit their daily or per-app limit.
- **New app installed** — A new app was installed and needs approval.
- **Uninstall attempt** — The child tried to remove Prime Kids.
- **Blocked content** — The child tried to access a blocked website or app.
- **Geofence entry/exit** — The child entered or left a safe zone.
- **Device offline** — The child's device went offline.
- **Unusual activity** — An unusual usage pattern was detected.
- **SOS emergency** — The child pressed the SOS button.

Alerts show the time, child name, and message. Repeated alerts within 30 minutes are grouped together. Use the "Mark all as read" button to clear your list.

### Multi-Child Support
- Manage multiple children from one parent account, each with their own:
  - Device and pairing
  - Screen time rules
  - App block lists
  - Web filter settings
  - Geofences
  - Activity reports

### Bilingual
- Full **English** and **Mongolian** language support.
- Switch languages anytime from Settings — the entire app updates instantly.

### Subscription Activation
- Activate with a simple key code in **PK-XXXX-XXXX** format.
- View days remaining, expiration date, and plan details.
- Renew anytime by entering a new key.

### Device Pairing
- **Simple 3-step setup:**
  1. Download the child app on the child's device.
  2. Generate a pairing code in the parent app.
  3. Enter the code on the child's device — done!
- Pairing code has a countdown timer so you know when it expires.
- The parent app detects pairing automatically — no need to refresh.

### Device Information
- See the child's device model, operating system version, and app version.
- Battery level with color-coded indicator (green, orange, red).
- Last seen timestamp showing when the device was last online.

### Notification Settings
- Toggle each alert type on or off individually.
- Set quiet hours to pause notifications during specific times (e.g. overnight).

### Account Management
- Edit your profile name.
- Change your password.
- Email verification.
- Delete your account if needed.

## Always-On Protection

- **Cannot be uninstalled** — The child cannot remove Prime Kids without the parent's permission. Any attempt triggers an alert to the parent.
- **Survives restarts** — The app automatically starts monitoring again after the device is rebooted.
- **Cannot be force-closed** — If the child tries to close the app, it restarts itself and continues protecting.
- **Fail-safe web filtering** — If the web filter is interrupted for any reason, the device blocks all internet access until the filter is restored. No unfiltered browsing is ever possible.
