Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Prime Kids ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Prime Kids: Parent Helper mobile application (the "Parent App") and the Prime Kids child companion application (the "Child App"), collectively referred to as the "Service." This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Parent/guardian name and email address
  • Password (stored as a salted hash; we never store plaintext passwords)
  • Child profiles: name and age

1.2 Device Information

  • Device model, platform (Android/iOS), and operating system version
  • App version installed on the child's device
  • Battery level and online/offline status
  • List of installed applications (package name and app name)
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

1.3 Activity & Usage Data

  • App usage duration per application
  • Web browsing URLs visited on the child's device
  • Blocked access attempts (apps, websites, uninstall attempts)
  • Screen time totals

1.4 Location Data

  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) from the child's device
  • Geofence entry and exit events
  • Reverse-geocoded address lookups (cached for up to 90 days)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Parental monitoring: to display child device activity, location, and screen time to the authorized parent/guardian
  • Rule enforcement: to enforce screen time limits, app restrictions, web content filters, and geofence alerts on the child's device
  • Alerts & notifications: to send real-time alerts (e.g., new app installed, blocked content, geofence triggers, SOS, device offline)
  • Service improvement: to maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service
  • Safety: to respond to SOS signals and ensure child safety

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using personal data depends on the data concerned and the context:

  • Consent: You provide explicit consent when creating an account and setting up child monitoring
  • Contract: Processing is necessary to provide the Service you have subscribed to
  • Legitimate interest: To maintain security and improve the Service

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following third-party service providers, solely to operate the Service:

  • Firebase (Google): Push notifications (FCM), crash reporting, and analytics. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps / Geocoding API: To convert GPS coordinates into human-readable addresses. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Email service provider: To send account verification and password reset emails.
  • MongoDB Atlas (if applicable): Cloud database hosting with encryption at rest.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as you maintain an active account.
  • Activity logs and location history are retained for up to 12 months, after which they are automatically purged.
  • When you request account deletion, all personal data is permanently erased within 30 days. During this grace period, you may contact us to cancel the deletion.
  • Geocoding cache entries expire automatically after 90 days.

6. Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds.
  • API access is protected by JWT authentication with short-lived access tokens and refresh tokens.
  • Rate limiting is applied to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Device authentication uses unique device tokens.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Prime Kids is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent or legal guardian. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Please see our COPPA Compliance Statement for full details.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • The Child App does not allow children to create accounts, enter personal information, or communicate with others.
  • All data collection on the child's device is initiated and controlled by the parent/guardian through the Parent App.
  • Parents may review, modify, or delete their child's data at any time through the Parent App or by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you or your child
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account
  • Restriction: Request that we limit the processing of your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@parenthelper.com.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including encryption and compliance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
