COPPA Compliance Statement

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Important: Prime Kids: Parent Helper is a parental monitoring tool designed for use by parents and legal guardians. The Service collects information from children's devices solely under the direction and control of the parent/guardian who sets up the monitoring.

1. Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

Prime Kids complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the FTC's COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children under 13 years of age.

2. How Our Service Works

Prime Kids is a two-app system:

  • Parent App: Installed on the parent/guardian's device. Only adults (18+) may create accounts. This app controls all monitoring settings.
  • Child App: Installed on the child's device by the parent/guardian. The child does not create an account, enter personal information, or interact with other users through this app.

3. Verifiable Parental Consent

We obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting any data from a child's device:

  • Only a verified parent/guardian account holder can add a child profile and pair a child's device.
  • The parent must physically install the Child App and complete the device pairing process, which requires entering a time-limited pairing code from the Parent App.
  • The parent must affirmatively grant device permissions (Location, Usage Stats, Device Admin, Accessibility) during setup.
  • This hands-on setup process constitutes verifiable parental consent as described in the FTC's COPPA guidance for parental monitoring tools.

4. Information Collected from Children's Devices

The following information is collected from a child's device, solely for parental monitoring purposes and only after verifiable parental consent:

4.1 Device Information

  • Device model, platform, and OS version
  • Battery level and connectivity status
  • List of installed applications

4.2 Activity Data

  • App usage durations
  • Web browsing history (URLs visited)
  • Blocked access attempts

4.3 Location Data

  • GPS coordinates (collected periodically)
  • Geofence entry/exit events

4.4 What We Do NOT Collect from Children

  • We do not collect children's names, email addresses, or contact information directly from the child. Child profile names are entered by the parent.
  • We do not collect photos, videos, or audio from the child's device.
  • We do not allow children to make their personal information publicly available.
  • We do not enable children to communicate with other users.
  • We do not use children's data for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. How Children's Data Is Used

Data collected from children's devices is used exclusively to:

  • Display activity reports and location to the authorized parent/guardian
  • Enforce parental rules (screen time limits, app blocks, web filters, geofences)
  • Send safety alerts to the parent (SOS, geofence triggers, device offline)

We do not use children's data for any commercial purpose unrelated to providing the Service. We do not serve behavioral advertising to children.

6. Data Sharing

Children's data is shared only with:

  • The authorized parent/guardian who set up monitoring
  • Third-party service providers necessary to operate the Service (Firebase for notifications, Google Maps for location display), under contracts that prohibit them from using the data for any other purpose

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose children's personal information to third parties for their own commercial purposes.

7. Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the right to:

  • Review: View all data collected from their child's device at any time through the Parent App (activity logs, location history, device info).
  • Delete: Remove a child's profile, which permanently deletes all associated data (activity logs, location history, device records, rules, alerts, and geofences).
  • Revoke consent: Unpair or uninstall the Child App at any time to stop all data collection.
  • Modify: Adjust monitoring settings, rules, and geofences at any time.
  • Request information: Contact us to request details about what data has been collected.

To exercise these rights, use the Parent App or contact us at support@parenthelper.com.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • When a child profile is deleted by the parent, all associated data is immediately and permanently removed (cascade deletion).
  • When a parent account is deleted, all child profiles and their data are permanently erased within 30 days.
  • Activity logs older than 12 months are automatically purged.

9. Data Security

We protect children's data with the same security measures applied to all user data:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens
  • Unique device tokens for child device authentication
  • Rate limiting and brute-force protection

10. GDPR Notice for EU Users

If you and your child are located in the European Union or European Economic Area:

  • We process children's data under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent of the holder of parental responsibility, per Article 8 GDPR).
  • You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your child's data.
  • You may lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.
  • For data subject requests, contact support@parenthelper.com.

11. Changes to This Statement

If we make material changes to how we collect or use children's data, we will notify parents via email and in-app notification and obtain new consent where required.

12. Contact Us

For questions about our COPPA compliance or children's privacy practices:

  • Email: support@parenthelper.com
